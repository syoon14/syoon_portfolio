Research projects

Now · Argonne · 2025–present

Cu(I) chromophores on metal-oxide quantum dots

I synthesize ZnO quantum dots and attach heteroleptic Cu(I) HETPHEN complexes to their surfaces to build donor–acceptor assemblies. I measure each complex’s redox potentials by cyclic voltammetry to test the thermodynamic driving force for charge transfer to ZnO, and we follow their charge transfer with transient absorption measured with collaborators. The goal is to learn how molecular structure controls the direction of charge transfer at the interface.

  • ZnO QDs
  • Cu(I) HETPHEN
  • cyclic voltammetry
  • interfacial charge transfer

Ph.D. · University of Houston · 2020–2024

Deep-red to near-infrared phosphors

I designed and made cyclometalated Ir(III) and Pt(II) complexes under air-free conditions, using the ancillary ligand to push emission into the deep red and near-infrared. Comparing red, deep-red and NIR series showed how ligand sterics and substituents control excited-state decay. The work produced deep-red phosphors with quantum yields up to 0.5 and a first-author JACS paper.

  • Ir(III)
  • Pt(II)
  • phosphorescence
  • ligand design

Earlier · 2017–2019

Materials and devices

Ni- and Pt-based conductive MOFs at Sandia National Laboratories, showing that metal substitution switches charge transport from n- to p-type. Metal–organic frameworks for dye removal from water at CSU Chico. Li-ion battery electrode formulation and cell testing at Nanotech Energy.

  • MOFs

  • Li-ion electrodes
